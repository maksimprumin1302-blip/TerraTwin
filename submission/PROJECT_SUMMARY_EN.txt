TerraTwin: Earth as a Space Laboratory

TerraTwin is a bilingual web application that helps students, engineers, and researchers explore Earth locations as potential testing environments for Moon and Mars exploration technologies. Users select a point on an interactive map, retrieve available climate, terrain, and geological information, and review six criteria: slope, surface materials, thermal extremes, water/ground-ice context, solar energy, and rover mobility. The application explains a project-defined suitability score and distinguishes measured or mapped data from model-derived estimates and missing evidence. This makes early-stage site screening easier to understand and more transparent, while showing why an Earth analog is never an exact planetary match. TerraTwin uses public scientific sources, including NASA POWER, and runs through an accessible Python/Streamlit interface. Future work includes deeper quantitative integration of lunar and Martian archives.
